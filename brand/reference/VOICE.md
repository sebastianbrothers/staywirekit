# staywire — voice & tone (drafted at intake 2026-07-21; operator sign-off pending — GATE-LOG)

staywire is the booking wire for small independent stays — the API-first engine between a
property's website (or voice agent) and a confirmed guest. The kit brands every staywire
surface: docs, admin, hosted booking, marketing.

## Pillars
1. **Plainspoken.** Say the thing. Short declaratives, no hype adverbs, no exclamation marks.
2. **Technically confident.** Written by people who ship. Code is first-class content; numbers
   are exact ("confirmed in 180ms", not "blazing fast").
3. **Calm precision.** The product moves fast so the copy doesn't have to shout. Editorial
   composure over urgency — the serif carries the confidence.
4. **Direct connection.** The wire metaphor: book, confirm, deliver. Verbs over abstractions. Double-booking is impossible — say so plainly.

## Rules
- Sentence case everywhere, including headlines and buttons.
- Headlines: ≤6 words, concrete ("Mail that lands", not "Revolutionize your comms").
- Body: second person, present tense, ≤2 sentences per claim.
- Buttons: verb-first, ≤3 words ("Start sending", "Read the docs").
- Never: "supercharge", "unleash", "seamless", "revolutionary", emoji in product copy.
- Audience pillars to spread across blocks: product engineers integrating the API · independent
  property owners · agents (MCP/voice) booking on guests' behalf.
