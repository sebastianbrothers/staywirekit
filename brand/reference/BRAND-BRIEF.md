# sendwire — brand brief (operator directive, 2026-07-21)

- **Brand name:** sendwire (new brand; no prior material exists anywhere in the studio — voice/identity derived at onboarding)
- **Visual inspiration:** https://resend.com/home — but as a LIGHT-MODE interpretation. Resend is dark-first; sendwire inverts to light-first. Layout, typography rhythm, and component shapes transfer; palette identity must be re-derived for light surfaces, not naively inverted.
- **Inspiration mode, not clone mode:** Resend is reference inspiration only. sendwire needs its own name/wordmark treatment, voice, and palette identity. RESEND-DESIGN.md (getdesign.md extraction) is the machine-readable reference.
- **Context:** kit consumed by the booking-platform product line.
